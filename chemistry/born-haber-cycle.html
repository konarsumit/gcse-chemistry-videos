Why the two routes must match

Hess's Law says the total enthalpy change only depends on the start and end points, not the path taken. So the direct route (elements → ionic solid, which is ΔHf) must equal the indirect route added together (atomisation + ionisation + atomisation of the non-metal + electron affinity + lattice enthalpy). If you know every step except one, you can always rearrange to find the missing value — that's exactly what the "Reveal" button above does.
