Four anomalies worth knowing: Be→B and Mg→Al dip because the outgoing electron now sits in a higher-energy p-subshell rather than a full s-subshell. N→O and P→S dip because the 4th p-electron must pair up in an already-occupied orbital, and that extra repulsion makes it easier to remove.
